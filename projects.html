HANGMAN GAME

This project is a multiplayer phrase-guessing game written in Java, where two players compete to reveal a hidden idiom or proverb by guessing letters and words. The game manages player scores, tracks revealed characters through a "Board" system, and awards points based on the accuracy of guesses until the phrase is fully solved.
